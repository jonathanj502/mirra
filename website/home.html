More help ↗
Can I choose what Mirra helps me with?+

Yes. Choose making friends, coming across more confidently, listening, clarity, or speaking up for yourself. Prefer no specific focus? Keep Everyday connection. Mirra saves your choice and uses it for new debriefs and Reflect replies. Change it from Your focus on the recording screen or Your conversation goal in Profile. No daily check-in needed.

How much time do I spend in the app?+

Just enough to start and stop a recording, let it upload, and read your short debrief. Mirra handles transcription and analysis. Reflect chat and progress views are optional; there’s no required daily check-in.

Is Mirra always listening?+

No. You choose when to start and stop each recording, with everyone’s permission. Mirra does not start recording automatically or listen all day.

Does everyone need to know I’m recording?+

Yes. Get everyone’s permission for recording and AI processing before you start. Mirra is for conversations people agree to share. Recording rules depend on where you and the other participants are.

Does Mirra know which voice is mine?+

Mirra estimates that the loudest speaker is you. It does not verify your identity from your voice. Keep the microphone near you and treat the results as estimates; noise and overlapping speech can affect them.

Can I use it without an internet connection?+

Once you’re signed in and have approved processing on your device, you can record offline. Stop and save before closing the app. Keep Mirra open when you reconnect so saved recordings can upload. AI analysis needs an internet connection.

When can I download it?+

Mirra is preparing for its first iPhone release, with Android to follow. Store downloads aren’t available yet. The first release includes five debriefs per month, with no paid plans.
